Profile

I like the part where a messy idea starts to make sense.

I’m a web designer and front-end developer working independently from Kuala Lumpur. I help founders, studios and small teams take websites and products from a rough sketch to something they’re glad to put their name on.

My favourite work sits between design and engineering — setting the type, finding the right words, then building the thing properly. I care how it sounds as much as how it looks, and I don’t think a website needs to shout to be noticed.
